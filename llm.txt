> Naya Nazimabad, a housing community in Karachi. Content lives in typed modules in `src/data/*.ts`.

## Conventions to remind the model of

- Content facts come from nayanazimabad.com or are marked unspecified. Never fabricate prices,
  possession dates or amenity details.
- Every new route needs metadata (`buildMetadata`), a sitemap entry, and JSON-LD where it fits.
- Anything inside `src/components/three/` must stay client-only and dynamically imported.
- Placeholders over stock photography; generated textures over image files.
